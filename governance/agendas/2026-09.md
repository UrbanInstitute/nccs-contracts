## Proposals from the chair

5. **Distinguish data we maintain from data we only archive or share.**
   Charter §3 distinguishes essential infrastructure from special
   projects outside its scope. Should we add a middle category for
   data we archive but do not curate or validate? The proposed
   categories are:

   - **Essential infrastructure:** data covered by our maintenance
     and governance commitments.
   - **Peripheral data:** actively archived, but not curated or
     validated, such as raw e-file XML and 990-N postcards.
   - **Project or external data:** shared by NCCS, but outside this
     committee's governance scope.

   Adoption would require a charter amendment.

6. **Make clear which charter clauses are requirements.** Label
   existing clauses as **principles** (aspirations), **guidance**
   (targets we do not monitor), or **requirements** (enforced
   obligations recorded in contracts). This would make the binding
   commitments easier to identify without rewriting the charter.
   Adoption would require a charter amendment.

7. **Settle three version-number conventions (charter §5).**

   - Should the first release of a wave use patch `00` or `01`?
   - Should stable datasets, such as crosswalks, keep a fixed wave
     number?
   - Should architecture version `A = 00` identify beta or
     experimental series?

8. **Should we add Census tracts to the address history? (BACKLOG Z15)**
   A doctoral researcher studying New York neighborhood organizations
   over time needs geography for past addresses. We committed on
   August 24 to notify her when the data is published.

   The proposal is to geocode each historical address period in the
   address-resolved crosswalk. This extends existing infrastructure
   and can reuse geocodes for the most recent addresses from the
   geocoded Unified BMF. Remaining addresses would be deduplicated
   before geocoding to avoid repeated work.

   Decide its priority under charter §8 and whether the geography
   belongs in a separate companion table or new crosswalk columns.
   If adopted, record the design in an architecture decision record
   (ADR).

9. **Record each dataset's governance policies in its contract.**
   Should contract YAMLs also specify collection and release
   schedules, protected variables, join keys, controlled vocabularies,
   privacy handling, and allowable uses? If adopted, document the
   schema change in a decision record.
